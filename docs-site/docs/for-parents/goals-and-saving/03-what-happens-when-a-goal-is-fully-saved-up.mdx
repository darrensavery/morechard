---
title: "What happens when a goal is fully saved up?"
---

<p>Once your child has saved enough (including any parent contributions), they'll see a <strong>Mark as Purchased</strong> button on the goal. Confirming it records the spend, closes out the goal as reached, and — if you have jars turned on — takes the amount out of their Save jar.</p>
