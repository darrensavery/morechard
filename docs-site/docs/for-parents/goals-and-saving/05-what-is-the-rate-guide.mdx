---
title: "What is the Rate Guide?"
---

<p>The Rate Guide is a searchable list of typical chore rewards, shown when you're creating a chore, to help you set a fair amount. It's filterable by category and sortable by price or popularity, based on what other families commonly pay for similar chores.</p>
