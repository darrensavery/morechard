<p>Yes, two ways. You can set an ongoing <strong>Parental Match</strong> percentage (0/10/25/50/100%) that effectively lowers how much your child needs to save themselves, or you can send a one-off <strong>gift contribution</strong> of a fixed amount. Either way, it's a real transaction - it's added to their saved total and recorded properly, not just a visual adjustment.</p>
