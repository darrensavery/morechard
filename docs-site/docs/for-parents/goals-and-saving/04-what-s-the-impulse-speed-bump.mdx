<p>It's a short "just checking" pause your child sees before logging a spend that's large relative to their balance (15%+ of what they have, and only if they have £5 or more). It doesn't block the spend - they can choose <strong>Wait a bit</strong> or <strong>I'm sure, log it</strong> - but it's designed to interrupt impulse purchases for a moment, and either choice is visible to you afterward.</p>
