<p>From their dashboard, your child taps <strong>New Goal</strong> and fills in a name, a target amount, a category (Toys, Games, Tech, Outing, Clothing, Books, or Other), and a save-by date. Older children (teen/Clean mode) can also attach a product link. Each child can have up to 5 active goals at once.</p>
