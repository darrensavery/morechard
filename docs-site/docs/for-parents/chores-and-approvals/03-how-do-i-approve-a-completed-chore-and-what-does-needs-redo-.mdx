<p>Chores waiting on you show up under <strong>Awaiting Review</strong> on your dashboard. You have three options:</p><ul><li><strong>Approve</strong> - marks it complete and writes the payment to your child's ledger immediately.</li><li><strong>Send back ("needs redo")</strong> - sends it back to your child with a note so they can fix it and resubmit. No payment happens yet, and it can go back and forth as many times as needed.</li><li><strong>Reject</strong> - closes it out with no payment. This one is final; your child would need to do the chore again from scratch next time it's due.</li></ul>
