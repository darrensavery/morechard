<p>From your parent dashboard, tap <strong>Create Chore</strong> and fill in:</p><ul><li><strong>Title</strong> - pick an icon or let Morechard suggest one</li><li><strong>Reward amount</strong> - what the chore pays when approved</li><li><strong>Frequency</strong> - One-off, Daily, Weekly, Fortnightly, Monthly, or School days</li><li><strong>Assign to</strong> - a specific child, several named children, "Anyone" (first to claim it gets paid), or "Everyone" (each child gets their own copy)</li></ul><p>You can also require a <strong>photo as proof</strong> before it's marked done, or turn on <strong>Skip Approval</strong> so it pays instantly the moment your child completes it. These two options are mutually exclusive - you can only pick one.</p><p>Not sure what to pay? Tap <strong>Rate Guide</strong> from the creation screen to see typical amounts other families pay for similar chores, filterable by category.</p>
