<p>Your child taps the chore on their dashboard and marks it done - if you required a photo, they'll attach one first. This moves the chore into <strong>Awaiting Review</strong>, where it sits until you approve it.</p><p>If you turned on <strong>Skip Approval</strong> for that chore, there's no waiting - it's marked completed and paid instantly, no review step.</p>
