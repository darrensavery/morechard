---
title: "What is the Ledger, and why does every entry get hashed?"
---

<p>The Ledger is Morechard's permanent record of what your child has earned and when. Every time you approve a chore, a new entry is added and cryptographically linked to the one before it — so if anyone tried to quietly edit or delete an old entry, the chain would visibly break.</p><p>Nothing is ever silently deleted. If a mistake needs correcting, a new linked entry reverses it — the original stays visible, just like a bank statement correction.</p><p>This matters most for co-parenting or separated families: it gives both parents one shared, tamper-evident record of exactly what was earned, ending arguments about "how much do I actually owe for that?" — see <strong>Exporting a court-ready PDF</strong> for how to share this record formally.</p>
