<p>Yes, and they're two separate things. <strong>Frequency</strong> (set when you create the chore) controls how often a fresh copy appears - daily, weekly, fortnightly, monthly, or on school days. <strong>Streaks</strong> track how many days in a row your child has kept up with their chores, as a bit of extra motivation - separate from payment and approval.</p>
