---
title: "How do push notifications work?"
---

<p>Push notifications are opt-in and only available in the phone app (not the web version). You'll be asked to allow them once, the first time they're relevant. You'll be notified for: a new chore assigned, a chore ready for your approval, a chore approved and paid, a chore sent back for redo, a savings goal getting boosted, and a child's request to give money to a cause.</p>
