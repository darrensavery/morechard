title: "What plans does Morechard offer, and what's included?"
---

<p>Morechard is sold as a one-time purchase, not a subscription:</p><ul><li><strong>Morechard Core — £44.99</strong>: the full chore tracker and ledger.</li><li><strong>Morechard Core AI — £64.99</strong>: everything in Core, plus AI Mentor and the Learning Lab.</li><li><strong>Morechard Shield AI — £149.99</strong>: everything in Core AI, plus the forensic-tier court-ready PDF export.</li><li><strong>AI Mentor upgrade — £29.99</strong>: adds AI Mentor and Learning Lab to a family that already bought Core.</li></ul><p>If you've already bought Core or the AI upgrade and later decide to get Shield AI, what you already paid is credited toward it — you only pay the difference.</p>
