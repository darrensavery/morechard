<p>Morechard has safeguards to stop the same purchase from being granted to your account twice, even if a payment gets processed more than once on our end. If you genuinely see two separate charges on your bank/card statement for the same purchase, email <a href="mailto:support@morechard.com">support@morechard.com</a> with both transaction details and we'll sort out a refund for the duplicate.</p>
