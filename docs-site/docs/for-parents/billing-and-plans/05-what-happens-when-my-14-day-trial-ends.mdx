---
title: "What happens when my 14-day trial ends?"
---

<p>Your trial starts the day your first chore is approved and runs for 14 days, during which every feature — including AI Mentor — is unlocked. If you haven't purchased a plan by the end of it, you'll get a usage summary email, and further use of the app will need a purchase to continue.</p>
