---
title: "How does the Payment Bridge work for paying my child?"
---

<p>Payment Bridge doesn't move money through Morechard itself — it helps you actually pay your child once you owe them. It opens your banking app (Monzo, Revolut, PayPal, or Venmo) with the amount and reference pre-filled, or gives you your child's bank details to copy for a manual transfer. Once you've sent it, you confirm "I've sent it" in the app so it's recorded.</p><p>Any bank details you save for this are kept only on your own device.</p>
