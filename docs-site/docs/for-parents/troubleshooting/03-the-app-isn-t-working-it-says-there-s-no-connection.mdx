<p>Morechard needs an internet connection for anything that reads or writes your family's data (approving chores, checking balances, etc.) - actions don't currently queue up to sync automatically once you're back online, so if something fails while you're offline, you'll need to try it again once your connection is back.</p><p>The app itself (the screens and navigation) will still open without a connection, since that part is cached on your device - it's specifically actions that need a live connection to work.</p>
