<p>A few things can explain this - check these first:</p><ul><li><strong>Wrong day.</strong> Recurring chores only appear on the days they're scheduled (e.g. a "school days" chore won't show on a Saturday).</li><li><strong>Assigned to a different child.</strong> Double-check who the chore is assigned to in Settings.</li><li><strong>It's overdue.</strong> A one-off chore past its due date moves into a separate overdue section rather than the main list.</li><li><strong>It was archived.</strong> An archived chore stops appearing entirely until restored.</li></ul><p>If none of these explain it, contact support and we'll check the assignment on our end.</p>
