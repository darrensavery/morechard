<p>Morechard updates itself automatically in the background, but a new version only takes effect the next time you fully close and reopen the app - a simple in-app refresh isn't enough while it's already running.</p><p>Try this: close the app completely (swipe it away from your recent apps, or close the browser tab entirely) and reopen it. On the web, a hard refresh (<span class="inline-code">Ctrl+Shift+R</span> on Windows, <span class="inline-code">Cmd+Shift+R</span> on Mac) works too.</p>
