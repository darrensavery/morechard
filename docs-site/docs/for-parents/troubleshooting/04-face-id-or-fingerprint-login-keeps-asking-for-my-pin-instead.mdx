<p>This usually means your device's saved biometric sign-in has been reset or invalidated — for example if you cleared your browser data, reinstalled the app, or changed your device passcode. Your account itself is fine — this only affects the device-level shortcut.</p><p>Use your PIN to get back in, then re-register biometrics from Settings if the option is available on your device. If it keeps happening, contact support.</p>
