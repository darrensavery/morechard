---
title: "How do I export a court-ready PDF, and what's the difference between the tiers?"
---

<p>From <strong>Settings → Data &amp; Exports</strong>, you can generate a PDF record of your child's Ledger. There are three tiers:</p><ul><li><strong>Basic</strong> — the ledger history and family/governance details. Available on every plan.</li><li><strong>Behavioral</strong> — adds status history and a record of which Learning Lab modules your child has unlocked. Requires AI Mentor.</li><li><strong>Forensic</strong> — the full audit trail: completions with GPS/device details, any edits made to chore rewards, and login history. Requires <strong>Shield AI</strong>.</li></ul><p>Every tier includes the cryptographic verification described below, so the document can be independently checked as unaltered.</p>
