---
title: "How do I invite my co-parent to our family account?"
---

<p>As the lead parent, go to your family setup and generate an invite code — it's a short code valid for 72 hours. Share it with your co-parent, and they'll enter it during their own sign-up to link into your family as a co-parent.</p><p>Only the lead parent can generate this code.</p>
