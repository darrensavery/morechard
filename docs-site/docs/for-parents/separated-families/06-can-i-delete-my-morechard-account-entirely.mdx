<p>Only the lead parent can delete ("uproot") a family account, and only once every co-parent has already left - this protects a co-parent from losing access without warning.</p><p>Deleting anonymizes all names, emails, and login details for everyone on the account and revokes every session. Your child's ledger history is <strong>not</strong> deleted - it's kept, anonymized, so financial records aren't lost even after the account closes.</p>
