---
title: "How do I remove a co-parent, or leave a family myself?"
---

<p>As the lead parent, you can remove a co-parent from your family at any time — this revokes their access and anonymizes their account details, but doesn't touch your child's ledger history.</p><p>If you're the co-parent and want to leave, you can do that yourself — if you were the only other adult on the account, another co-parent (if any) is automatically promoted to lead.</p>
