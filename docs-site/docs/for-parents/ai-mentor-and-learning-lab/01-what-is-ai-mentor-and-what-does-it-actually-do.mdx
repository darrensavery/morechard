<p>AI Mentor watches for patterns in your child's real activity — spending too fast, their balance sitting at zero, chores going overdue, money just piling up unused — and sends them a short, timely nudge when something's worth pointing out. It's behavioral coaching triggered by real events, not a fixed lesson schedule.</p><p>AI Mentor is included with Morechard Core AI, Shield AI, or the AI Mentor upgrade — Core-only families see an upsell instead. Everyone gets full access during their 14-day trial, regardless of plan.</p>
