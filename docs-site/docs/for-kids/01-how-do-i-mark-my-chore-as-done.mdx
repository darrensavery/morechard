---
title: "[For kids] How do I mark my chore as done?"
---

<p>Tap the chore on your dashboard and hit <strong>Done</strong>. If your parent asked for a photo, take one first — that's your proof it's finished.</p><p>Some chores pay you straight away. Others go to your parent to check first — you'll see it sitting under <strong>Waiting</strong> until they look at it.</p>
