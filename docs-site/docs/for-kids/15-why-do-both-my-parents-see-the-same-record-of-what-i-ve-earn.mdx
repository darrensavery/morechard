---
title: "[For kids] Why do both my parents see the same record of what I've earned?"
---

<p>If your parents live apart, they both still see exactly the same record of your chores and earnings — it's shared, not split or different for each of them. That way nobody's guessing or arguing about what you've actually earned; you only have one true record, and both parents see it.</p>
