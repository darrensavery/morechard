---
title: "[For kids] How do notifications work for me?"
---

<p>If you're using the phone app, you can get a notification when something happens that involves you — like when a new chore is assigned to you, or when one you finished gets approved and paid. You'll be asked once whether to allow them; you can always change your mind later in your phone's settings.</p>
