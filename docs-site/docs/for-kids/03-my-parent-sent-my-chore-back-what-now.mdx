---
title: "[For kids] My parent sent my chore back — what now?"
---

<p>If a chore comes back to you, your parent wants something fixed or finished before they can pay you for it. There's usually a note explaining what to change. Fix it and send it again — you can go back and forth as many times as you need, and you won't lose anything by trying again.</p>
