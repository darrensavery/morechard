---
title: "[For kids] Can my parents see everything I do in the app?"
---

<p>Your parents can see what you've earned, saved, and spent — that's how they approve chores and help with goals. What they can't see is anything private like your exact location — proof photos are only used to check a chore was actually done, not to track where you are.</p>
