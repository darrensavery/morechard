---
title: "[For kids] Why does my chore come back every week?"
---

<p>If your parent set a chore to repeat — daily, weekly, or on school days — a fresh one shows up automatically each time, so you don't need to ask for it again. Doing it several times in a row can also build up a streak, which is just a way of tracking how consistent you've been.</p>
