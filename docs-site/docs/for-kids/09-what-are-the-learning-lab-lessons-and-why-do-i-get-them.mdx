<p>Learning Lab is a set of short lessons about money - things like saving, spending wisely, and how interest works. You don't do them in order; a new one unlocks when something you've actually done (like reaching a savings goal, or going a while without spending) makes that lesson useful right then. Each one only takes about 15 - 25 minutes: a quick intro, a short lesson, something to try, and a quiz at the end.</p><p>Not every family has Learning Lab turned on - if you don't see it, that's just how your account is set up, not something broken.</p>
