---
title: "[For kids] Why did the app ask me to wait before I spent money?"
---

<p>If you're about to spend a big chunk of what you have, Morechard shows you a quick "just checking" screen before you go ahead. It's not stopping you — you can still choose <strong>I'm sure, log it</strong> — it's just a moment to think it over first in case you'd rather wait.</p>
