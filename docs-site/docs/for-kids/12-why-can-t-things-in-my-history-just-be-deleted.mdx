---
title: "[For kids] Why can't things in my history just be deleted?"
---

<p>Every chore you get paid for is added to a permanent record that can't be secretly changed or erased later — think of it like a diary nobody's allowed to tear pages out of. If something needs correcting, a new entry is added to fix it, but the original stays visible too. It's there so what you've earned is always fair and never in dispute.</p>
