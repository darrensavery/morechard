---
title: "[For kids] What happens when I finish saving up for my goal?"
---

<p>Once you've saved enough, a <strong>Mark as Purchased</strong> button appears on your goal. Tapping it confirms you've bought (or are about to buy) the thing you saved for, and closes the goal out as done. It's there to celebrate finishing, not to nag you into spending — you can leave a fully-saved goal alone as long as you like.</p>
