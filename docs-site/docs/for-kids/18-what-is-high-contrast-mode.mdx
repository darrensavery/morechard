---
title: "[For kids] What is High Contrast mode?"
---

<p>It's a display setting that makes text and buttons stand out more clearly, which can make the app easier to read. You can turn it on or off yourself in Appearance settings — it doesn't change anything about your account, just how it looks.</p>
